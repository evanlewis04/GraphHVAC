# Graph Wiki

This vault is the living operational wiki for the distributor. Every page is
generated and maintained by Graph's compilation and agent loops; treat
hand-edits as temporary unless you also update the source data.

## Sections

- [[skus/]] — every SKU in the catalog
- [[suppliers/]] — supplier relationships and concentration notes
- [[customers/]] — customer accounts, segments, and purchase patterns
- [[warehouses/]] — locations, inventory snapshots
- [[refrigerants/]] — refrigerant types, regulatory status, exposure analysis
- [[concepts/]] — cross-cutting analyses (tariff impact, phase-out exposure, etc.)

## How answers are sourced

Every answer the chat produces cites the wiki pages it read and the SQL
queries it ran. Click "Show sources" in the chat to see the full trace.
