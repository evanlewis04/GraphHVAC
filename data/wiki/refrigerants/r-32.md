# R-32

## regulatory_status

R-32 (difluoromethane) has a GWP of 675 (AR4), which places it below the EPA Technology Transitions Rule threshold of GWP < 700 [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]. This makes it a compliant refrigerant for new residential and light-commercial AC and heat-pump equipment manufactured on or after January 1, 2025 [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc].

R-32 is classified A2L (mildly flammable) under ASHRAE standards. This classification triggers charge-limit requirements per UL 60335-2-40 and additional installer training requirements, though it is not considered dangerous in normal residential charges [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]. A2L-rated tools and leak detectors are required for service in confined spaces [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd].

R-32 is not subject to the AIM Act bulk phase-down in the same manner as R-410A (GWP ~2088), though HFC allowances broadly tighten through 2036+ [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc].

## retrofit_options

R-32 is **not** a drop-in retrofit for R-410A systems. It is a single-component refrigerant (no glide), runs approximately 5–10% higher saturation pressure than R-410A, and delivers roughly 5–10% higher capacity [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]. Mixing R-32 and R-410A in the same system destroys the system; strict bulk-inventory separation is required at the distributor level [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd].

R-32 is positioned as a new-equipment refrigerant, not a service drop-in for existing R-410A installs. Contractors transitioning installed-base customers should be offered replacement-equipment quotes on R-32 or R-454B equivalents [sop-r-410a-retrofit-customer-outreach:0000:2349bfb54c].

**OEM adoption as of 2024-Q4:**

- **Primary R-32 platforms:** Daikin (long-standing), Mitsubishi, parts of the Goodman/Amana lineup [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd].
- **Partial R-32 adoption:** Rheem, York, and some Lennox commercial lines [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7].
- **R-32 SKUs in current pricebooks:** Trane commercial condensers (e.g., TRA889, TRA515, TRA768) [spec-trane-2024-commercial-r-32:0000:531e8bb392]; Carrier 2025 residential condensers (e.g., CAR512, CAR987, CAR610) [spec-carrier-2025-residential-condensers:0000:730402755b]; Lennox 2025 condensers (e.g., LEN252, LEN792, LEN556) [spec-lennox-2025-condenser-pricebook-excerpt:0000:2675d0b6a1]; Rheem 2025 condensers (e.g., RHE177, RHE272, RHE348) [spec-rheem-2025-r-454b-condensers:0000:bb87b1f4eb].

Line-set reuse is permitted on new R-32 installs if lengths are within OEM spec, the line set is free of moisture and acid, and it has been triple-flushed per OEM procedure [manual-synth-install-residential-condenser:0000:dc2822e656].

Forward buys on R-32 are not currently offered by this distributor; supply is not constrained as of 2024-Q3 [sop-refrigerant-pre-buy-guidance:0000:75588b7bfe].

## exposure_summary

R-32 exposure for distributors is primarily a **stocking and tooling readiness issue**, not a supply-constraint risk. Key points:

- Distributors must stock two replacement refrigerants in parallel through the transition (R-32 and R-454B), since OEMs are split between the two [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd].
- A2L-rated recovery machines, gauges, and leak detectors are required for R-32 service calls. Brazing torches remain allowed under manufacturer-specified procedures [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]. Same tooling requirements apply to R-454B, so a single A2L tool investment covers both replacement refrigerants [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7].
- Minimum room-volume requirements for the evaporator location apply under UL 60335-2-40 — relevant for retrofit sizing conversations with contractors [manual-synth-install-residential-condenser:0000:dc2822e656].
- Contractors with heavy R-410A purchase history should be targeted for outreach and offered R-32 (or R-454B) replacement quotes proactively [sop-r-410a-retrofit-customer-outreach:0000:2349bfb54c].
- Unlike R-410A, R-32 pricing is currently high but not supply-allocated; no forward-buy contracts are warranted at this time [sop-refrigerant-pre-buy-guidance:0000:75588b7bfe].
