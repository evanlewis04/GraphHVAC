# R-410A

## regulatory_status

R-410A (GWP ~2088, ASHRAE A1 non-flammable) is directly affected by two separate tracks under the [[regulations/aim-act]].

**Bulk supply phase-down:** The [[regulations/aim-act]] places R-410A under the HFC allowance schedule, which drops to 60% of the 2011–2013 baseline for 2024–2028, then 30% starting 2029 [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc]. Allowances are auctioned annually; tighter supply each year pushes bulk pricing higher [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc].

**Equipment manufacture cutoff:** The EPA Technology Transitions Rule prohibits new manufacture and import of R-410A residential and light-commercial AC and heat-pump equipment effective **January 1, 2025** (GWP < 700 threshold) [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc][bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973]. VRF equipment follows by January 1, 2026 [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc].

**What remains legal:** Service refills of R-410A on existing equipment are still permitted after the manufacture cutoff. Distributor inventory held at 2024-12-31 may continue to be sold and installed [bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973]. However, refill pricing was already up ~18% year-over-year heading into the transition, and the allowance schedule tightens further through 2028 [sop-r-410a-retrofit-customer-outreach:0000:2349bfb54c].

Expect reclaimed-refrigerant programs to cover a growing share of service supply from 2027 onward as allowance pressure intensifies toward the 30% baseline floor [bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973].

## retrofit_options

New 2025+ residential and light-commercial equipment ships in one of two low-GWP replacements — [[refrigerants/r-454b]] or [[refrigerants/r-32]] — depending on OEM:

| Replacement | GWP | ASHRAE class | Key OEM adopters |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[refrigerants/r-454b]] | 466 | A2L | [[manufacturers/carrier]], [[manufacturers/trane]], [[manufacturers/lennox]], [[manufacturers/bryant]] (primary); [[manufacturers/rheem]], [[manufacturers/york]] (partial) [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7] |
| [[refrigerants/r-32]] | 675 | A2L | [[manufacturers/daikin]], [[manufacturers/mitsubishi]], parts of [[manufacturers/goodman]] / [[manufacturers/amana]] lineup [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd] |

Neither refrigerant is a drop-in for R-410A. Both are A2L (mildly flammable), which requires A2L-rated leak detectors, recovery machines, and gauges [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]. [[refrigerants/r-454b]] is a blend with ~1.5 °F glide and **must be charged as a liquid** to avoid fractionation — gaseous charging from a virgin cylinder will not maintain blend composition [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]. [[refrigerants/r-32]] is single-component (no glide), runs ~5–10% higher saturation pressure than R-410A, and offers ~5–10% more capacity [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd].

Line set reuse is conditionally permitted: the existing set must be within OEM length spec, free of moisture and acid, and triple-flushed per OEM procedure for A2L systems [manual-synth-install-residential-condenser:0000:dc2822e656].

Mixing R-32 and R-410A in the same system destroys the system; strict separation of bulk inventory is mandatory [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd].

For the step-by-step field process, see [[procedures/r-410a-retrofit-workflow]].

## exposure_summary

R-410A exposure is a financial and operational planning concern for contractors and distributors carrying significant R-410A-tied revenue into the post-2025 service cycle.

**Identifying exposure:** The recommended query flags customers with R-410A purchases exceeding $15K over the trailing 24 months, sizing a call list of roughly 50 customers per region [sop-r-410a-retrofit-customer-outreach:0000:2349bfb54c]. A tighter pre-buy eligibility screen targets customers with 24-month total revenue > $50K **and** R-410A revenue share > 30% [sop-refrigerant-pre-buy-guidance:0000:75588b7bfe].

**Pricing trajectory:** Bulk R-410A prices are projected to climb 20–60% over 2025–2026 as AIM Act allowances compress [bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973]. [[refrigerants/r-454b]] is currently running 20–35% above R-410A on a per-pound basis [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7].

**Pre-buy policy:** Eligible customers may pre-buy up to 18 months of trailing R-410A consumption, capped at 200 25-lb cylinders per customer, priced at spot + 8%. Storage billing ($0.85/cylinder/month) kicks in after month 2; distributor does not hold unallocated inventory for pre-buy [sop-refrigerant-pre-buy-guidance:0000:75588b7bfe]. A 50-cylinder pre-buy inquiry was logged in Q4 2024 for a [[manufacturers/lennox]] customer at WH-CHI [ticket-contractor-notes-2024-q4#TKT-2024-Q4-004:0000:bdfda6993c].

**Distributor actions:** Tag every SKU's refrigerant type in ERP for one-step exposure queries; triage R-410A SKUs for draw-down vs. stockpile by Q4; conduct proactive outreach to R-410A-heavy contractors with replacement quotes [bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973][sop-r-410a-retrofit-customer-outreach:0000:2349bfb54c]. [[manufacturers/lennox]] terminal R-410A finished-goods inventory was flagged at 62 units as of early 2025, with no further production planned [email-lennox-2025-q1-allocations:0000:848d0c2bdb].
