# R-410A

## regulatory_status

R-410A (GWP ~2088, ASHRAE A1) is subject to two overlapping EPA regulatory mechanisms under the AIM Act.

**Bulk phase-down.** The AIM Act phases down U.S. HFC production and import against a 2011–2013 baseline. R-410A supply is constrained under the 60% allowance window running 2024–2028, tightening further to 30% in 2029–2033 and 15% by 2036+. [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc]

**Equipment manufacture cutoff.** The EPA Technology Transitions Rule prohibits new manufacture and import of residential and light-commercial AC and heat-pump equipment using refrigerants with GWP ≥ 700 effective **January 1, 2025**. R-410A does not qualify for new equipment under this threshold. [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc][bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973]

**Service refills remain legal.** Topping off existing R-410A systems is still permitted after the 2025 cutoff. [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc][bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973] However, allowance compression is already pushing refill prices up — approximately 18% year-over-year as of 2024, with further increases expected through 2028. [sop-r-410a-retrofit-customer-outreach:0000:2349bfb54c]

R-410A inventory held by distributors at 2024-12-31 may continue to be sold and installed. [bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973]

## retrofit_options

There is no drop-in retrofit for R-410A — replacement requires new equipment. The two primary low-GWP successors for residential/light-commercial are:

**R-454B (Opteon XL41 / Solstice 454B)**
- GWP 466, ASHRAE A2L (mildly flammable). [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]
- Primary platform for Carrier, Trane, Lennox, and Bryant 2025+ model years. [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7][email-lennox-2025-q1-allocations:0000:848d0c2bdb]
- Capacity approximately equivalent to R-410A; saturation pressure ~3–5% lower. [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]
- **Must be charged as liquid** to prevent fractionation — the single most common service error during transition. [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]
- Pricing currently 20–35% above R-410A per pound. [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]
- A2L-rated tools, leak detectors, and training required. [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]

**R-32 (difluoromethane)**
- GWP 675, ASHRAE A2L (mildly flammable); single-component, no glide. [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]
- Capacity ~5–10% higher than R-410A; saturation pressure ~5–10% higher. [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]
- Primary platform for Daikin, Mitsubishi, and parts of the Goodman/Amana lineup. [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]
- Same A2L service requirements as R-454B. [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]
- **Critical:** mixing R-32 and R-410A in the same system destroys the system. Strict bulk inventory separation required. [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]

**Line set reuse:** permitted on new R-454B/R-32 installations if lengths are within OEM spec, the line set is free of moisture and acid, and it has been triple-flushed per OEM procedure. [manual-synth-install-residential-condenser:0000:dc2822e656]

**Pre-buy option:** contractors with heavy R-410A service exposure can lock current pricing via pre-buy contracts (eligible if 24-month revenue > $50K and R-410A revenue share > 30%), capped at 200 25-lb cylinders per customer. [sop-refrigerant-pre-buy-guidance:0000:75588b7bfe]

## exposure_summary

"Exposure" in this context refers to distributor and contractor business risk tied to R-410A-dependent revenue.

- Contractors with high R-410A installed base face rising service costs that are increasingly difficult to pass on, given the multi-year allowance compression running through 2028. [sop-r-410a-retrofit-customer-outreach:0000:2349bfb54c]
- Distributors should run 24-month revenue queries to identify customers where R-410A revenue exceeds $15K — these are the priority outreach targets for replacement quotes and pre-buy conversations. [sop-r-410a-retrofit-customer-outreach:0000:2349bfb54c]
- Lennox terminal R-410A residential inventory was down to 62 finished-goods units as of early January 2025; no further production planned. [email-lennox-2025-q1-allocations:0000:848d0c2bdb]
- R-410A bulk prices are expected to climb 20–60% between 2025 and end of 2026 as AIM Act allowances hold at 60% of baseline through 2028. [bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973]
- Reclaimed-refrigerant programs are expected to supply a larger share of the R-410A service market starting around 2027 as virgin supply tightens further. [bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973]
- Distributor ERP tagging of all R-410A SKUs (via a `skus.refrigerant` column) is recommended to keep exposure queries operationally accessible. [bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973]
