# R-454B

R-454B (trade names Opteon XL41, Solstice 454B) is the leading low-GWP replacement for [[refrigerants/r-410a]] in U.S. residential AC and heat-pump equipment. It is an A2L-classified blend composed of 68.9% [[refrigerants/r-32]] and 31.1% [[refrigerants/r-1234yf]], with a GWP of 466 versus R-410A's 2088 [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7].

## regulatory_status

R-454B qualifies under the [[regulations/aim-act]] Technology Transitions Rule GWP < 700 threshold for residential and light-commercial AC and heat pumps, which took effect **January 1, 2025** [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc]. [[refrigerants/r-410a]] (GWP ~2088) does not meet this threshold; new manufacture and import of R-410A residential equipment is prohibited from that date forward [bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973]. R-454B is therefore a compliant drop-in platform refrigerant for new equipment. Variable refrigerant flow (VRF) equipment follows a separate GWP < 700 cutoff phasing in by January 1, 2026 [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc].

Service refills of R-410A in existing equipment remain legal after the 2025 cutoff, but bulk allowances compress through 2028 (60% of baseline) and tighten further to 30% starting 2029, driving ongoing price pressure on legacy refrigerants [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc][bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973]. R-454B supply is currently not constrained by allocation; forward-buy programs are not warranted at this time [sop-refrigerant-pre-buy-guidance:0000:75588b7bfe].

## retrofit_options

**R-454B is not a drop-in retrofit for existing R-410A systems.** For [[manufacturers/trane]]'s 4TWR8 platform (the first Trane residential heat pump built on R-454B), a customer with an existing R-410A 4TWR7 unit must perform a full system replacement: condenser + evaporator + line set flush + new TXV. Line set reuse is permitted only if it meets OEM length and condition specs and is triple-flushed per OEM procedure [bulletin-trane-tsb-2024-002-r-32-launch:0000:d16e4c2761][manual-synth-install-residential-condenser:0000:dc2822e656].

For distributors, the practical retrofit workflow is equipment replacement, not refrigerant conversion. The [[procedures/r-410a-retrofit-workflow]] covers the customer outreach and quote process for contractors with R-410A-heavy installed bases [sop-r-410a-retrofit-customer-outreach:0000:2349bfb54c].

**OEM platform availability for R-454B replacements:**

- [[manufacturers/carrier]] and [[manufacturers/bryant]] — primary residential platform [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]
- [[manufacturers/trane]] — 4TWR8 series; 2025 model year production [bulletin-trane-tsb-2024-002-r-32-launch:0000:d16e4c2761]
- [[manufacturers/lennox]] — primary platform across residential condenser line [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7][spec-lennox-2025-condenser-pricebook-excerpt:0000:2675d0b6a1]
- [[manufacturers/rheem]] and [[manufacturers/york]] — partial adoption; some lines on [[refrigerants/r-32]] instead [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]
- [[manufacturers/goodman]] and [[manufacturers/amana]] — primarily R-32; some R-454B SKUs in commercial lines [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]

**Key service tool requirements for R-454B installs:** A2L-rated leak detector, A2L-rated digital manifold gauge set, A2L-rated recovery machine with R-454B blend-preservation rating, and a liquid-line charging hose (3/8" SAE) [bulletin-trane-tsb-2024-002-r-32-launch:0000:d16e4c2761]. Nitrogen pressure test target is 350–400 psig for R-454B systems (lower than R-410A's 500–550 psig) [manual-synth-install-residential-condenser:0000:dc2822e656].

**Critical charging note:** R-454B must be charged as a liquid from the tank. Vapor charging from a virgin cylinder will fractionate the blend due to the ~1.5 °F temperature glide. This is the single most common service mistake during the transition [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7][bulletin-trane-tsb-2024-002-r-32-launch:0000:d16e4c2761].

## exposure_summary

R-454B is the dominant new-equipment refrigerant across [[manufacturers/carrier]], [[manufacturers/trane]], and [[manufacturers/lennox]] 2025 residential lines [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]. Distributor stocking units are 25-lb cylinders as the standard SKU through 2025; 100-lb returnable cylinders are expected from primary suppliers mid-2025. Current pricing runs 20–35% above R-410A on a per-pound basis [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7].

Field demand is already active — contractor inquiries for 25-lb R-454B cylinder availability are occurring at warehouse level [ticket-contractor-notes-2024-q4#TKT-2024-Q4-009:0000:fc69e53e57]. Unlike R-410A, R-454B is not subject to AIM Act allocation constraints at this time, so forward-buy contracts are not offered; revisit if supplier allocation notices emerge [sop-refrigerant-pre-buy-guidance:0000:75588b7bfe].

Distributors stocking R-454B alongside [[refrigerants/r-32]] must maintain strict bulk inventory separation — the two refrigerants are not interchangeable, and mixing in a system causes equipment damage [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]. Recovered R-454B retains blend composition (small glide) but must be reclaimed, not reused on-site [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7].
