# R-454B

## regulatory_status

R-454B has a GWP of 466 (AR4), which places it well below the EPA Technology Transitions Rule threshold of GWP < 700 for new residential and light-commercial AC and heat-pump equipment [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]. That cutoff took effect **January 1, 2025** — R-410A (GWP ~2088) no longer qualifies for new manufacture [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc]. R-454B and R-32 are the two primary compliant replacements cited by EPA [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc].

R-454B is ASHRAE class **A2L** (mildly flammable), which triggers charge-limit requirements under UL 60335-2-40 for the evaporator location [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7][manual-synth-install-residential-condenser:0000:dc2822e656].

The AIM Act bulk phase-down runs separately from the equipment cutoff: HFC allowances drop to 60% of baseline through 2028, then 30% from 2029–2033 [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc]. This compresses R-410A supply over time but does not restrict R-454B production or import.

## retrofit_options

**R-454B is not a drop-in retrofit for R-410A systems.** Existing R-410A equipment cannot be converted to R-454B in the field. For example, Trane's 4TWR8 (R-454B) explicitly cannot be retrofitted from the 4TWR7 (R-410A) — the required path is full system replacement: new condenser, new evaporator, line set flush, and new TXV [bulletin-trane-tsb-2024-002-r-32-launch:0000:d16e4c2761].

Line set reuse is conditionally permitted if lengths are within OEM spec, the line set is free of moisture and acid, and it has been triple-flushed per OEM procedure [manual-synth-install-residential-condenser:0000:dc2822e656][bulletin-trane-tsb-2024-002-r-32-launch:0000:d16e4c2761].

**OEM platform availability:** R-454B equipment is available across major residential and commercial lines:

- **Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Bryant** — primary platform for 2025+ model years [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]
- **Rheem and York** — partial adoption [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]
- **Goodman/Amana** — primarily R-32; some R-454B SKUs in the commercial line [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]

Contractors with R-410A-heavy installed bases should be quoted R-32 or R-454B equivalents on a full system-replacement basis [sop-r-410a-retrofit-customer-outreach:0000:2349bfb54c].

**Required service tools for R-454B work:**

- A2L-rated leak detector
- A2L-rated digital manifold gauge set (required for charge weight verification)
- A2L-rated recovery machine with R-454B blend-preservation rating
- Liquid-line charging hose (3/8" SAE) — **vapor charging from a virgin cylinder will fractionate the blend** [bulletin-trane-tsb-2024-002-r-32-launch:0000:d16e4c2761]

The liquid-charging requirement is the most common service mistake during the transition. R-454B has a ~1.5 °F temperature glide; charging as vapor causes fractionation [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7].

## exposure_summary

**Refrigerant pricing:** R-454B is currently running 20–35% above R-410A on a per-pound basis. The standard distributor SKU is the 25-lb cylinder; larger 100-lb returnable packaging became available mid-2025 from primary suppliers [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7].

**Supply outlook:** Forward buys on R-454B are not currently offered because supply is not constrained — pricing is elevated but there are no supplier allocation notices on file (as of 2024-Q3) [sop-refrigerant-pre-buy-guidance:0000:75588b7bfe]. This contrasts with R-410A, where pre-buy contracts are actively managed due to AIM Act allowance compression.

**Charge quantities (Trane 4TWR8 reference):** R-454B factory charges run slightly higher than the equivalent R-410A charges — for example, a 3.0-ton unit takes 7 lb 4 oz of R-454B vs. 7 lb 0 oz of R-410A [bulletin-trane-tsb-2024-002-r-32-launch:0000:d16e4c2761]. Factor this into cylinder pull quantities at the counter.

**A2L handling:** Same class as R-32 — mildly flammable. Does not present unusual hazard at normal residential charge sizes, but A2L-rated tools and technician training are required [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7][bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd].
