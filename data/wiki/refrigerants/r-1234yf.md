## retrofit_options

R-1234yf is a component refrigerant used in blends such as R-454B (68.9% R-32 / 31.1% R-1234yf), which has been adopted as a low-GWP replacement for R-410A in residential AC and heat-pump equipment by major OEMs including Carrier, Trane, Lennox, and Bryant for 2025+ model years [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]. R-454B carries an ASHRAE A2L (mildly flammable) classification and a GWP of 466 versus R-410A's 2088 [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7].
