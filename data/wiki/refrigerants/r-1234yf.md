## Retrofit Options

R-1234yf is a component blend constituent rather than a standalone drop-in retrofit refrigerant in the residential HVAC market. It makes up 31.1% of [[refrigerants/r-454b]] (trade name Opteon XL41 / Solstice 454B), which is the leading low-GWP replacement for [[refrigerants/r-410a]] adopted by [[manufacturers/carrier]], [[manufacturers/trane]], [[manufacturers/lennox]], and [[manufacturers/bryant]] for 2025+ residential AC and heat-pump lines [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7].

Because R-454B contains R-1234yf as a component, technicians servicing R-454B equipment should be aware that the blend carries an A2L (mildly flammable) ASHRAE classification, requiring leak detectors and A2L-rated tools [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]. Liquid charging is mandatory for R-454B to preserve blend composition — gaseous charging from a virgin cylinder will cause fractionation of the R-1234yf/[[refrigerants/r-32]] ratio [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7].

Partial adoption of R-454B (and thus R-1234yf-containing blends) is also seen at [[manufacturers/rheem]] and [[manufacturers/york]], while [[manufacturers/goodman]] and [[manufacturers/amana]] have taken primarily the pure [[refrigerants/r-32]] path with select R-454B commercial SKUs [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7].
