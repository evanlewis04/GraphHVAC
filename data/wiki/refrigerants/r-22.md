# R-22

## Regulatory Status

R-22 (HCFC-22) production and import for use in new equipment was banned in the United States as of January 1, 2010; virgin production and import was phased out entirely by January 1, 2020 [sop-refrigerant-pre-buy-guidance:0000:75588b7bfe]. The [[regulations/aim-act]] and [[regulations/epa-snap]] framework governs the broader HFC and HCFC phasedown landscape in which R-22 was an early casualty. Only recovered, reclaimed, or stockpiled R-22 may be sold for servicing existing equipment [sop-refrigerant-pre-buy-guidance:0000:75588b7bfe].

From a distributor inventory standpoint, R-22 is treated as a fully constrained, reclaim-only commodity. No pre-buy or forward-buy contracts are offered on R-22, as the policy reflects its end-of-production status [sop-refrigerant-pre-buy-guidance:0000:75588b7bfe].

## Exposure Summary

Because R-22 supply is limited to reclaimed product, price volatility and allocation risk are significant for contractors still servicing legacy equipment. Customers carrying R-22-dependent service portfolios should be evaluated for transition planning toward [[refrigerants/r-410a]] or next-generation low-GWP alternatives such as [[refrigerants/r-454b]] and [[refrigerants/r-32]] [sop-refrigerant-pre-buy-guidance:0000:75588b7bfe]. Equipment manufacturers including [[manufacturers/carrier]], [[manufacturers/trane]], [[manufacturers/lennox]], [[manufacturers/rheem]], and [[manufacturers/goodman]] ceased production of R-22 systems well over a decade ago, meaning the installed base is aging and retrofit or replacement decisions are increasingly urgent. No distributor-level pre-buy or stocking commitment on R-22 is supported given supply constraints [sop-refrigerant-pre-buy-guidance:0000:75588b7bfe].
