# Bryant

## Overview

Bryant is an HVAC equipment manufacturer with an active product line spanning residential and commercial heating and cooling equipment. The brand appears across multiple U.S. distributor warehouses (including WH-PHX, WH-CHI, and WH-CHA), indicating broad national distribution [ticket-contractor-notes-2024-q3#TKT-2024-Q3-002:0000:9c86e28d3f][ticket-warranty-claims-2024#TKT-MS-2024-009:0000:d6801a3406][ticket-warranty-claims-2024#TKT-MS-2024-001:0000:1b9cb72b8e]. Bryant issues its own Technical Service Bulletins (TSBs) addressing field failure modes and recommended part replacements [bulletin-bryant-tsb-2024-008-inducer-motor-bearing:0000:87189af649]. Standard parts warranty is 5 years [bulletin-bryant-tsb-2024-008-inducer-motor-bearing:0000:87189af649].

## Regulatory Posture

Bryant is identified as a primary adopter of **R-454B** (A2L, GWP 466) for U.S. residential AC and heat-pump platforms in the 2025+ model year, alongside Carrier, Trane, and Lennox [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]. This aligns Bryant with the EPA R-410A phase-out trajectory, under which new R-410A equipment manufacture ends January 1, 2025 [sop-r-410a-retrofit-customer-outreach:0000:2349bfb54c].

Field service on Bryant's A2L equipment requires A2L-rated tools, leak detectors, and technician training — the same requirements that apply to all A2L-class refrigerants [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7][ticket-contractor-notes-2024-q3#TKT-2024-Q3-002:0000:9c86e28d3f]. Liquid charging is mandatory for R-454B to prevent fractionation [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7].
