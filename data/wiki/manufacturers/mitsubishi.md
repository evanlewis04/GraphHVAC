## regulatory_posture

Mitsubishi has adopted **R-32** as its refrigerant of choice for residential models in the U.S. market as of 2024-Q4, aligning with Daikin and parts of the Goodman/Amana lineup rather than the R-454B path taken by Carrier, Trane, and Lennox [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd].

R-32 carries an ASHRAE A2L (mildly flammable) classification with a GWP of 675 versus R-410A's 2088, placing Mitsubishi equipment within the A2L regulatory and installation framework [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]. Field implications for servicing Mitsubishi R-32 equipment include:

- A2L-rated leak detectors required in confined spaces
- A2L-rated recovery machines and gauges
- Charge limits per UL 60335-2-40
- Additional installer training requirements [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]

Contractor inquiries confirm R-454B cylinders are also being requested at Mitsubishi-heavy warehouses (WH-CHA), likely for mixed-fleet service needs [ticket-contractor-notes-2024-q4#TKT-2024-Q4-003:0000:e903bee253]. Counter staff should verify refrigerant type per specific equipment model before fulfilling orders.
