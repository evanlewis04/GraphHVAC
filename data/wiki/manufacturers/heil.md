# Heil

## Overview

Heil is an HVAC equipment manufacturer with a recurring presence across distributor counter activity and technical support channels. Ticket records from 2024–2025 show Heil-branded equipment (model series HEI202, HEI543, HEI669, HEI736, HEI961) serviced across multiple warehouses including WH-CHI and WH-CHA [ticket-contractor-notes-2024-q3#TKT-2024-Q3-005:0000:9194e5650d][ticket-contractor-notes-2024-q4#TKT-2024-Q4-005:0000:e6e86fdddb][ticket-counter-day-2025-q1#TKT-2025-Q1-003:0000:b2fca43ac7]. Heil is a brand under the broader [[manufacturers/carrier]] family of HVAC manufacturers, competing in the residential and light-commercial segment alongside brands such as [[manufacturers/bryant]] and [[manufacturers/goodman]].

## Product Categories

Heil's product portfolio, as evidenced by distributor ticket activity, spans at minimum:

- **Residential HVAC systems** — Multiple HEI-series model numbers (HEI202, HEI543, HEI669, HEI736) appear across counter and contractor ticket queues, indicating a broad equipment lineup supported at the parts distribution level [ticket-contractor-notes-2024-q3#TKT-2024-Q3-005:0000:9194e5650d][ticket-contractor-notes-2024-q4#TKT-2024-Q4-006:0000:29192bc2d2][ticket-counter-day-2025-q1#TKT-2025-Q1-003:0000:b2fca43ac7].
- **Smart thermostats** — The HEI961 is a Wi-Fi-enabled smart thermostat with cloud connectivity and OTA firmware update capability. A known issue with Wi-Fi auto-reconnect after router reboot is addressed by firmware update HE-64562, delivered OTA or via manual USB [bulletin-heil-tsb-2024-009-thermostat-wifi-reconnect:0000:f5a4b1cf64].
