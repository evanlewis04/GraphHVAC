# US Motors

## Overview

US Motors is a motor manufacturer whose product line is stocked by this distributor for HVAC replacement and OEM applications. The brand appears in the Baker 2025 catalog under the **Motor** product type, covering blower motor SKUs across a range of horsepower ratings and voltages [spec-baker-2025-blower-motors:0000:f79b341223]. US Motors competes in the same replacement motor segment as [[manufacturers/marathon]] and [[manufacturers/fasco]], making it a common cross-reference option when sourcing ECM or PSC blower motors for equipment from [[manufacturers/carrier]], [[manufacturers/trane]], [[manufacturers/lennox]], and others.
