# Amana

## regulatory_posture

Amana (as part of the Goodman/Amana lineup) has aligned primarily with **R-32** as its low-GWP replacement refrigerant for residential equipment, with some R-454B SKUs present in the commercial line [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd][bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]. R-32 carries an ASHRAE A2L (mildly flammable) classification, which drives charge-limit requirements per UL 60335-2-40 and additional installer training [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd].

Field contractors are actively requesting A2L service training kits for Amana equipment, indicating the transition to A2L refrigerants is already hitting the installed base [ticket-counter-day-2025-q1#TKT-2025-Q1-005:0000:12bf609454][ticket-contractor-notes-2024-q3#TKT-2024-Q3-009:0000:d26e149143]. Servicing A2L-charged Amana units requires A2L-rated leak detectors, recovery machines, and gauges [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd].

On the R-410A phase-out: EPA's manufacture cutoff for new R-410A equipment took effect 2025-01-01; service refills remain legal but refill pricing has risen significantly and the allowance schedule tightens through 2028 [sop-r-410a-retrofit-customer-outreach:0000:2349bfb54c]. Contractors with Amana R-410A installed base should be engaged on replacement-quote and pre-buy options accordingly [sop-r-410a-retrofit-customer-outreach:0000:2349bfb54c].
