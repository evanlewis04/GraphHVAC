# Lennox

## Overview

Lennox is a major residential and light-commercial HVAC OEM distributed through its Lennox PartsPlus channel. The distributor relationship is managed via a dedicated regional account manager structure (e.g., Southeast RAM Marcus Hale, parts rep Greg Ostrowski) with purchasing contact routed through `purchasing@<our-domain>` [email-lennox-2025-q1-allocations:0000:848d0c2bdb][email-lennox-partsplus-2024-05-18-delivery-delay-006:0000:1ea8eafe42].

Lennox PartsPlus is listed among the major suppliers tracked for volume-tier rebate consolidation alongside Carrier Enterprise, Trane Supply, Johnstone Supply, and RE Michel [sop-supplier-consolidation-post-mna:0000:014bd6862b]. An annual Loyalty rebate program is offered; the 2025 program enrollment opened December 2024 with tier thresholds and accrual basis unchanged from 2024 [email-lennox-partsplus-2024-12-12-rebate-program-028:0000:91840d0548].

## Product Categories

**Residential Condensers** are the primary stocked category. The 2025 pricebook covers models ranging from 1.5 to 5.0 tons across R-410A, R-454B, and R-32 refrigerant platforms [spec-lennox-2025-condenser-pricebook-excerpt:0000:2675d0b6a1]. Lennox transitioned the bulk of its 2025 model-year residential condenser line to R-454B; key models include the ML17XC1/ML18XC1 and XP25 heat pump [email-lennox-2025-q1-allocations:0000:848d0c2bdb]. R-410A terminal inventory (e.g., EL16XC1) remains available in limited finished-goods quantity [email-lennox-2025-q1-allocations:0000:848d0c2bdb].

**Smart Thermostats / Controls** are also supplied, specifically the iComfort S30 (model 10F81), a Wi-Fi-connected thermostat with cloud monitoring via the LennoxPros app [bulletin-lennox-tsb-2024-001-icomfort:0000:854994a674].

**Motors** are stocked as a parts category; shipments originate from the Charlotte facility [email-lennox-partsplus-2024-05-18-delivery-delay-006:0000:1ea8eafe42].

**A2L service training kits** are available for model LEN819 (field query on record) [ticket-counter-day-2025-q1#TKT-2025-Q1-008:0000:e7786e66cc].

## Regulatory Posture

Lennox has aligned to **R-454B** as its primary low-GWP refrigerant for the 2025 residential line, consistent with the EPA AIM Act equipment transition away from R-410A [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd][email-lennox-2025-q1-allocations:0000:848d0c2bdb]. This makes Lennox an R-454B house, not R-32 — verify this on every order since the two refrigerants are not interchangeable [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd].

R-454B is an A2L (mildly flammable) refrigerant, which drives A2L-rated tooling and installer training requirements at the service level [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]. Counter staff have fielded questions about A2L training kits for Lennox equipment [ticket-counter-day-2025-q1#TKT-2025-Q1-008:0000:e7786e66cc].

Pricing on R-454B units increased 3.5% effective 2025-02-01, attributed to aluminum/Section 232 tariff pass-through plus a refrigerant cost component [email-lennox-2025-q1-allocations:0000:848d0c2bdb]. R-410A terminal inventory carries no further production behind it; once finished goods are exhausted, no reorder is possible [email-lennox-2025-q1-allocations:0000:848d0c2bdb].
