# AHRI-210-240

## Summary

AHRI 210/240 is the Air-Conditioning, Heating, and Refrigeration Institute performance rating standard for unitary air-conditioning and heat-pump equipment. It defines the test procedures and conditions under which residential and light-commercial unitary equipment is rated for efficiency (SEER2, EER2, HSPF2 under the current revision). The standard is the technical backbone behind DOE minimum-efficiency regulations and is directly referenced in the [[regulations/aim-act]] Technology Transitions Rule, which sets GWP-based refrigerant cutoffs for equipment categories covered by AHRI 210/240 [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc].

Equipment rated under AHRI 210/240 includes the residential and light-commercial AC and heat-pump lines manufactured by [[manufacturers/carrier]], [[manufacturers/trane]], [[manufacturers/lennox]], [[manufacturers/rheem]], [[manufacturers/goodman]], [[manufacturers/amana]], [[manufacturers/york]], [[manufacturers/bryant]], [[manufacturers/heil]], [[manufacturers/daikin]], and [[manufacturers/mitsubishi]], among others [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd][bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7].

> **Note:** No source chunks in the current corpus contain the full AHRI 210/240 standard text. Claims here are drawn from regulatory and transition bulletins that reference the equipment categories governed by the standard.

## Effective Dates

The most operationally significant date tied to AHRI 210/240-rated equipment is **January 1, 2025**, when the [[regulations/aim-act]] Technology Transitions Rule prohibited new manufacture and import of residential and light-commercial AC and heat-pump equipment using refrigerants with GWP ≥ 700 — eliminating [[refrigerants/r-410a]] (GWP ~2088) from new production [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc][bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973].

Key dates affecting AHRI 210/240-covered equipment:

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2024-12-31 | Last day R-410A residential/light-commercial AC and HP equipment may be manufactured or imported [bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973] |
| 2025-01-01 | New OEM model years ship in [[refrigerants/r-32]] or [[refrigerants/r-454b]] [bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973] |
| 2026-01-01 | VRF systems (a separate AHRI-rated category) must meet GWP < 700 cutoff [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc] |

R-410A already in distributor possession at 2024-12-31 may continue to be sold and installed; service refills of existing equipment remain legal beyond 2025 [bulletin-r-410a-phase-out-timeline:0000:ef1c9a2973][bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc].

## Affected Equipment

AHRI 210/240 covers **residential and light-commercial unitary air-conditioning and heat-pump equipment** — the category subject to the January 1, 2025 GWP < 700 manufacture restriction under the [[regulations/aim-act]] [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc].

Practically, this means every split-system and packaged AC/HP unit from OEMs now transitioning to low-GWP refrigerants:

- **[[refrigerants/r-454b]]-platform lines:** [[manufacturers/carrier]], [[manufacturers/trane]], [[manufacturers/lennox]], [[manufacturers/bryant]], with partial adoption by [[manufacturers/rheem]] and [[manufacturers/york]] [bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]
- **[[refrigerants/r-32]]-platform lines:** [[manufacturers/daikin]], [[manufacturers/mitsubishi]], and the primary [[manufacturers/goodman]]/[[manufacturers/amana]] residential lineup [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd]

Variable refrigerant flow (VRF) systems follow a separate GWP cutoff timeline (2026) and are not covered under AHRI 210/240 proper [bulletin-epa-aim-act-summary:0000:2219085edc].

Both [[refrigerants/r-32]] and [[refrigerants/r-454b]] carry an ASHRAE A2L (mildly flammable) classification, driving charge-limit requirements under UL 60335-2-40 that affect how AHRI 210/240-rated equipment is installed and serviced going forward [bulletin-r-32-transition-overview:0000:29e90451bd][bulletin-r-454b-overview:0000:5abf4e6fa7]. Distributors stocking these lines should reference the [[procedures/r-410a-retrofit-workflow]] for field transition guidance.
