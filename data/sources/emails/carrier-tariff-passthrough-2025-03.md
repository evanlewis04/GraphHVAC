# Carrier Enterprise — 2025-03 tariff pass-through

---

**From:** Diane Yu <dyu@carrierenterprise.example>
**To:** purchasing@graph-distributor.example
**Date:** 2025-03-02 11:08 EST
**Subject:** 2025-03 price adjustment — Section 301 pass-through

Hi all,

Heads up: effective **2025-03-17** Carrier will pass through a **4.2%
increase** across the residential condenser, heat pump, and packaged
unit lines. This is tied to the Section 301 China tariff increase
announced in February and applies specifically to lines with controls
content sourced from Tianjin (most of the 24ABC6, 24ANB7, 25HCC5
families).

Specifically affected:

- 24ABC6 series — all tonnages
- 24ANB7 series — all tonnages
- 25HCC5 series — 2.0–5.0 ton
- 38MGRQ packaged units — partial (verify per model number)

Furnaces, evaporator coils, and the 25VNA8 series are **not** affected
in this round. We expect a separate update on the coil line within
60 days.

Updated price book lands by 2025-03-10. POs in flight at the old
price will be honored at the old price provided they ship by 2025-03-31.

Reach out with questions.

Diane
Carrier Enterprise | National Accounts
