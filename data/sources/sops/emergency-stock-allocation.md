# Emergency stock allocation between warehouses

Owner: Operations. Internal SOP.

## Section 1

Inter-warehouse transfers are used to address regional stock-out. Transfer is initiated by warehouse manager request to purchasing.

## Section 2

Approval is automatic for SKUs with < 4 days of regional stock. Above that threshold, VP Ops approval required.

## Section 3

Transfer cost is allocated 50/50 between origin and destination warehouses for the current period.

## Section 4

Receiving warehouse must confirm receipt within 48h or the transfer auto-reverses.
