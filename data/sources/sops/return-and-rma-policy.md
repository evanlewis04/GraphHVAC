# Return and RMA policy

Owner: Operations. Internal SOP.

## Section 1

Counter returns within 30 days, original receipt, original packaging — full credit.

## Section 2

Counter returns 30–60 days — restocking fee (15%) at counter manager discretion.

## Section 3

Beyond 60 days — case-by-case; default deny.

## Section 4

Refrigerant returns are not accepted under any circumstance unless an unopened cylinder from a clearly damaged shipment is returned within 14 days of delivery.

## Section 5

Special-order SKUs (non-stock, brought in for a specific job) are non-returnable except for warranty defect.
