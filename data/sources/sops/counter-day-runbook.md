# Counter day runbook

Owner: Operations. Internal SOP.

## Section 1

Counter opens 7am, closes 5pm at all warehouses except Phoenix (6am open).

## Section 2

Pre-open: counter staff confirms cash drawer, prints day's open POs, verifies high-demand SKU on-hand against the morning replenishment.

## Section 3

Standard transactions: cash sale, account sale (charge to customer account), pickup of online order, warranty swap, return.

## Section 4

Edge cases: refrigerant sale (record EPA 608 cert # on receipt), credit-hold customer (direct to billing), refused delivery (return to receiving), allocated SKU (see allocation log before promising delivery).
