# New customer onboarding

Owner: Operations. Internal SOP.

## Section 1

New contractor customer signs the account application; provides EIN, certificate of insurance, EPA 608 certification (or specifies they will buy through a certified tech).

## Section 2

Credit application: trade references (3), bank reference, requested credit limit.

## Section 3

Approval flow: standard for credit < $25K; CFO sign-off for > $25K.

## Section 4

Onboarding session: counter walkthrough, online portal demo, account rep introduction, first-order discount code.

## Section 5

Tag customer in CRM with segment (residential / commercial / property mgr / builder) and region. Drives reporting in the daily exec brief.
