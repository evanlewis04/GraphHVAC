# Customer concentration review

Owner: Operations. Internal SOP.

## Section 1

Quarterly review of customer revenue concentration by VP Sales + CFO.

## Section 2

Metrics: top-10 share of TTM revenue, top-25 share, single-customer maximum share.

## Section 3

Risk thresholds: top-10 > 35% or single customer > 7% triggers an action plan.

## Section 4

Action plans: targeted outreach to mid-tier customers to grow their share, evaluation of segment exposure (e.g. heavy concentration in property managers), and contract terms review on the largest accounts.
