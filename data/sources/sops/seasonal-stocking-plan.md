# Seasonal stocking plan

Owner: Operations. Internal SOP.

## Section 1

Pre-cooling-season (Mar–Apr) stocking targets: 10 weeks of cooling-side SKU demand (capacitors, contactors, condenser fans, R-410A and R-454B refrigerant).

## Section 2

Pre-heating-season (Sep–Oct) stocking targets: 8 weeks of heating-side SKU demand (igniters, gas valves, inducer motors, furnace IFC boards).

## Section 3

Stocking levels are set at the per-warehouse level using a regional demand model.

## Section 4

Override: warehouse manager can request +/- 15% from purchasing without VP Ops approval if local conditions warrant (e.g. early heat wave forecast).
