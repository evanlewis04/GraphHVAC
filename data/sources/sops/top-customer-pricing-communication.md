# Top customer pricing communication template

Owner: Operations. Internal SOP.

## Section 1

Triggered by any pricing change affecting a top-25 customer's commonly-purchased SKUs.

## Section 2

Channel: direct call from account rep, followed by an email confirming the conversation.

## Section 3

Content: effective date, magnitude, the substitute path if one exists, an offer for a pre-buy at the old price if the customer has unfulfilled commitments.

## Section 4

Cadence: same-day for tariff and allocation-driven changes; within 3 days for routine supplier price changes.

## Section 5

Log every conversation in CRM with timestamp and customer reaction.
