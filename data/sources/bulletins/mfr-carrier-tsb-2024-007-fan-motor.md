# Carrier Technical Service Bulletin 2024-007

**Subject:** 24ABC6 series condenser — replacement procedure for
condenser fan motor (HC39GE237).

**Affected models:** 24ABC6 series, all tonnages. Production dates 2021
through current.

**Severity:** Service. No safety issue. Common service repair.

## Symptom

Condenser fan motor (Carrier P/N HC39GE237, replaces obsolete
HC39GE234) reaches end of bearing life. Typical failure mode is
intermittent stall at startup, progressing to locked rotor and tripped
high-pressure cutout.

Audible: bearing growl on startup, increasing over weeks; followed by
no-start with high-pressure trip.

## Replacement procedure (summary)

1. Lock-out / tag-out service disconnect.
2. Remove top grille (8x 5/16" hex screws).
3. Disconnect fan motor leads at terminal strip — note color/terminal
   mapping before removal. Common (white), Run (brown), Start (purple),
   capacitor (purple/white-stripe).
4. Cut zip ties on lead bundle.
5. Lift fan assembly. Mark blade orientation before removing motor —
   blade must reinstall in same direction or unit will short-cycle on
   high pressure.
6. Remove 4x motor mounting bolts. Slide motor out of blade hub.
7. Install replacement HC39GE237. Match blade orientation. Verify
   blade-to-shroud clearance per service manual table 4-7
   (0.250"–0.375").
8. Reconnect leads per terminal strip mapping.
9. Verify run capacitor MFD rating matches motor spec (typically 5 MFD
   for HC39GE237; verify on motor nameplate).
10. Restore power, verify rotation, verify amp draw within nameplate
    spec.

## Replacement parts cross-reference

- **Motor:** Carrier HC39GE237. Aftermarket cross: see
  [[manufacturers/US-Motors]] equivalent listings; refer customer to
  counter staff for compatibility.
- **Run capacitor:** Carrier P291-5054R (5 MFD, 440V). See

- **Fan blade:** Carrier LA01EB203 (do not reuse if visibly bent or
  cracked).

## Distributor notes

Recommend stocking 4–6 units of HC39GE237 per warehouse during cooling
season. Aftermarket-equivalent motors are widely available and 30–45%
lower cost; Carrier warranty disposition for non-OEM replacement is
denial — communicate this to contractors.
