# Mitsubishi Technical Service Bulletin 2024-007

Subject: 35 MFD / 440V dual-run capacitor — voluntary replacement of batch MI-94537.

Symptom: a subset of capacitors from production batch MI-94537 were measured at 8–12% under rated capacitance out of bag. Symptom in the field is hard-start on the compressor and compressor short-cycling.

Resolution: replace any in-stock units from the affected batch. Free replacement program open through end-of-quarter. Old units returned per standard RA process; do not discard.

Warranty: full credit for any installed unit failing within 90 days; standard 1-year on all replacements.
