# R-32 — replacement refrigerant overview

R-32 (difluoromethane) is one of two leading low-GWP replacements for
R-410A in U.S. residential AC and heat-pump equipment. The other is
R-454B; see [[bulletins/r-454b-overview]].

## Properties

| Property                 | R-32          | R-410A     |
| ------------------------ | ------------- | ---------- |
| ASHRAE classification    | A2L (mildly flammable) | A1 (non-flammable) |
| GWP (AR4)                | 675           | 2088       |
| Single-component         | Yes           | No (blend) |
| Glide                    | None          | Low        |
| Approx. capacity vs R-410A | +5–10%      | baseline   |
| Saturation pressure      | ~5–10% higher | baseline   |

A2L classification matters: it is mildly flammable. This drives charge
limits per UL 60335-2-40 and additional installer training requirements.
It does **not** mean the refrigerant is dangerous in normal residential
charges.

## OEM adoption

As of 2024-Q4, U.S. OEMs are split between R-32 and R-454B for
residential models:

- **R-32 adopters:** Daikin (long-standing), Mitsubishi, parts of the
  Goodman/Amana lineup.
- **R-454B adopters:** Carrier, Trane, Lennox, parts of Rheem and York.

Distributor implications: stocking two replacement refrigerants in
parallel through the transition. Verify per-line OEM choice on every
supplier order.

## Servicing concerns

- A2L-rated leak detectors required in confined spaces.
- Recovery machines and gauges must be A2L-rated.
- Brazing torches still allowed but require manufacturer-specified
  procedures.
- Mixing R-32 and R-410A in the same system **destroys** the system.
  Strict separation of bulk inventory required.
