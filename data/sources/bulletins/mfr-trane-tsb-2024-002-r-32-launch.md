# Trane Technical Service Bulletin 2024-002

**Subject:** 4TWR8 series heat pump — R-454B model launch. Distributor
stocking, service tool, and crossover guidance.

**Effective:** Production starts 2024-Q3 for 2025 model year.

## What's new

The 4TWR8 platform is the first Trane residential heat-pump line built
on R-454B. The 4TWR7 series (R-410A) continues in production through
end-of-life inventory burn-down in 2024-Q4.

Model number changes from `4TWR7XXX1000A` &rarr; `4TWR8XXX1000A`. Tonnages
and electrical specs are unchanged. Refrigerant charge differs:

| Tonnage | 4TWR7 (R-410A) charge | 4TWR8 (R-454B) charge |
| ------- | --------------------- | --------------------- |
| 2.0     | 5 lb 8 oz             | 5 lb 12 oz            |
| 2.5     | 6 lb 4 oz             | 6 lb 8 oz             |
| 3.0     | 7 lb 0 oz             | 7 lb 4 oz             |
| 3.5     | 7 lb 12 oz            | 8 lb 0 oz             |
| 4.0     | 9 lb 0 oz             | 9 lb 4 oz             |
| 5.0     | 10 lb 8 oz            | 10 lb 12 oz           |

## Service tool requirements

Contractors servicing the 4TWR8 must have:

- A2L-rated leak detector (per UL 60335-2-40 §GG.6.2.4).
- A2L-rated digital manifold gauge set. Mechanical gauges may still be
  used for pressure-only checks but the digital set is required for
  charge weight verification.
- A2L-rated recovery machine with R-454B blend-preservation rating.
- Liquid-line charging hose (3/8" SAE). **Mandatory** — vapor-charging
  R-454B from a virgin cylinder will fractionate the blend.

## Crossover from R-410A service

4TWR8 cannot be retrofitted from R-410A. If a customer has an R-410A
4TWR7 and needs replacement, the path is full system replacement
(condenser + evaporator + line set flush + new TXV). Reuse of the line
set is permitted only after a triple-flush per Trane service procedure
PM-454B-001.

## Distributor stocking notes

- Reduce 4TWR7 orders for 2024-Q4 to inventory-on-hand needs only.
- Begin stocking 4TWR8 in 2024-Q3 paired with R-454B 25-lb cylinders.
- A2L-rated tool starter kits available from Trane Supply (P/N
  KIT-R454B-START); we recommend bundling with first 4TWR8 sale to
  any contractor who has not previously serviced A2L equipment.
