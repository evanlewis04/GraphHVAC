# R-454B — replacement refrigerant overview

R-454B (trade name Opteon XL41, Solstice 454B) is the leading low-GWP
replacement chosen by Carrier, Trane, and Lennox for U.S. residential
AC and heat-pump equipment in 2025+ model years.

## Properties

| Property                 | R-454B          | R-410A     |
| ------------------------ | --------------- | ---------- |
| ASHRAE classification    | A2L (mildly flammable) | A1 (non-flammable) |
| GWP (AR4)                | 466             | 2088       |
| Composition              | 68.9% R-32 / 31.1% R-1234yf | 50/50 R-32/R-125 |
| Glide                    | ~1.5 °F         | <0.3 °F    |
| Approx. capacity vs R-410A | ~equivalent   | baseline   |
| Saturation pressure      | ~3–5% lower     | baseline   |

The small temperature glide means R-454B must be charged as a liquid
(out of the tank in liquid phase) to avoid fractionation. This is the
single most common service mistake during the transition.

## OEM adoption

- **Carrier**, **Trane**, **Lennox**, **Bryant** — primary platform.
- **Rheem** and **York** — partial adoption; some lines on R-32.
- **Goodman/Amana** — primarily R-32; some R-454B SKUs in the commercial line.

## Servicing concerns

Same A2L-class requirements as R-32 (leak detectors, A2L-rated tools,
training). Additionally:

- **Liquid charging is mandatory** — gaseous charging from a virgin
  cylinder will not maintain blend composition.
- Recovered R-454B retains blend composition because of the small glide
  but should still be reclaimed (not reused on-site).

## Distributor stocking notes

R-454B 25-lb cylinders are the standard distributor SKU through 2025;
larger packaging (100-lb returnable) appears mid-2025 from primary
suppliers. Pricing is currently 20–35% above R-410A on a per-pound
basis but converges as volume scales.
